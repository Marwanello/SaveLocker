PlayStation console icons

The PlayStation logo with the generation's number beside it, as EmulationStation themes draw it, in place of the controller glyph PS1–PS4, PSP and Vita use today. The logo's outline is the CC0 path from Simple Icons 16.35.0, not drawn by eye.

1 · The mark, at the sizes SaveLocker draws it

One colour, drawn in the text colour around it, like every other console glyph. The number sits top right, as in the theme. PS1 has no number, as on the original box.

3 · Coloured tiles, for a game with no cover art

The theme's look: each generation's colour, the logo lit and shadowed. Here they stand in for missing box art on the Games wall and in the console's grid. Small places (chips, badges) keep the one-colour mark from section 1.

4 · To decide

PSP and Vita

The theme has no number for them. Shown here as the logo with a short “PSP” / “Vita” beside it. The alternative is the logo alone, which would look the same as PS1.

Trademark

Simple Icons' path data is CC0, but the PlayStation logo is Sony's trademark. Using it to name the console a save belongs to is the same use EmulationStation themes and RetroArch make. It would be recorded in NOTICE-icons.md next to the other sources.
