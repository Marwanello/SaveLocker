PlayStation and Xenia icons

Each PlayStation console as the PlayStation logo with its own number or name beside it, as EmulationStation themes draw it, in place of the controller glyph PS1–PS4, PSP and Vita use today. Every shape is real path data: the logo and the digits come from Simple Icons 16.35.0 (CC0), cut out of each console's own wordmark, so the 2 is the PS2's 2. One colour throughout, like every other console glyph.

Trademarks

Simple Icons' path data is CC0, but the PlayStation logo and wordmarks are Sony's trademarks. They would be used only to name the console a save belongs to, as EmulationStation themes and RetroArch do, and recorded in NOTICE-icons.md next to the other sources.

Xenia's credit

CC BY-SA 4.0, like Arcticons: credited in NOTICE-icons.md, and the traced glyph is shared under the same licence.

Small sizes

At 14–16 px the number is about 6 px tall. The PS3, PS4 and Vita glyphs still read; the PS2's 2 and the PSP wordmark are hairlines in the consoles' own fonts and fade at chip size. Either keep them true to the fonts (the chip's text names the console anyway), or thicken just those two slightly below 24 px.
